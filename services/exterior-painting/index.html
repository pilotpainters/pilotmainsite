Exterior services

Exterior paint that stands up to Chicagoland weather

We focus on adhesion and drainage details—so your repaint lasts longer than a quick coat and a hope.

Get a Free Quote

What is included

  • Wash and mildew care

    A clean surface is the foundation—chalking and organic growth are handled before coatings go on.

  • Surface restoration

    Scraping, sanding, and spot repairs address peeling and bare spots so coatings bond properly.

  • Prime where it counts

    Stain-blocking and bonding primers go where bare wood or repairs need extra insurance.

  • UV-aware color

    We help you choose colors and sheens that look great in full sun and hold depth over time.

  • Sealed edges and trim

    Careful prep at joints and trim reduces moisture intrusion and premature peeling.

How we approach exterior painting

  1. 1

    Site review and protection

    We walk the elevation, note trouble spots, and protect landscaping, walks, and windows.

  2. 2

    Clean and prep

    Washing, scraping, sanding, and repairs create a sound surface for primer and paint.

  3. 3

    Coatings application

    We apply manufacturer-recommended systems with consistent mil thickness and sharp transitions.

  4. 4

    Detail pass and cleanup

    Final inspection, touch-ups, and jobsite cleanup—before we call it done.
