How we approach cabinet refinishing

  1. 1

    Plan and protection

    We map doors and drawers, protect adjacent surfaces, and confirm the finish level you want.

  2. 2

    Prep and prime

    Clean, sand, and prime for a uniform foundation—this is where smoothness is won or lost.

  3. 3

    Spray finish and cure

    Topcoats are applied in controlled passes with recoat windows respected for durability.

  4. 4

    Walkthrough and care notes

    We reinstall hardware, review touch points, and share simple care guidance for the first week.

What is included

  • Degrease and clean

    Residue is removed so primer and topcoats bond—especially around pulls and high-touch areas.

  • Sanding and profiling

    Mechanical prep opens the surface for a mechanical bond without fuzzy grain.

  • Primer system matched to substrate

    We choose bonding primers suited to your cabinet material and existing coating.

  • Controlled spray environment

    Masking and airflow control help deliver a consistent, low-texture finish.

  • Reassembly and QC

    Hardware goes back aligned; we inspect edges and panels in natural light before handoff.
