Interior services

Interior painting that feels intentional, not rushed

We plan color flow room-to-room, prep surfaces the right way, and leave edges sharp—so your home looks refreshed without the stress of a messy remodel.

Get a Free Quote

What is included

  • Color consultation

    We help you compare undertones and sheens so the palette feels cohesive from entry to bedrooms.

  • Low-VOC coatings

    Premium low-odor options keep air quality comfortable while work is underway in occupied homes.

  • Prep that lasts

    Patching, sanding, caulking, and primer where it matters—so the topcoat looks smooth and holds up.

  • Lighting-aware finish

    We check color in natural and artificial light to avoid surprises after the crew wraps.

  • Trim and doors

    Clean lines on casing, base, and doors—details that make a repaint feel brand new.

How we approach interior painting

  1. 1

    Walkthrough and scope

    We confirm rooms, surfaces, sheens, and schedule—plus how we will protect floors and belongings.

  2. 2

    Prep and prime

    Masking, repairs, and spot priming create a uniform base for color and sheen.

  3. 3

    Coatings and detail

    We apply consistent coverage and cut in carefully around trim, outlets, and transitions.

  4. 4

    Final look and touch-ups

    A walkthrough together ensures touch-ups are handled before we reset furniture and remove protection.
