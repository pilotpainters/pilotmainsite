How we approach commercial painting

  1. 1

    Scope and safety review

    We confirm hours, access, PPE needs, and protection for fixtures, flooring, and signage.

  2. 2

    Prep and repairs

    Patching, caulking, and primer address high-traffic wear before color goes on.

  3. 3

    Application windows

    Coating schedules align with dry times, occupancy, and any landlord requirements.

  4. 4

    Closeout and turnover

    Touch-ups, label-back of colors, and a clean handoff for facilities teams.

What is included

  • Phased scheduling

    We can sequence areas to keep corridors usable and reduce downtime in active suites.

  • After-hours options

    When needed, we align shifts so customer-facing areas stay presentable during business hours.

  • Clear site communication

    A single point of contact and daily expectations keep property teams in the loop.

  • Ventilation planning

    We coordinate airflow and low-odor systems suited to occupied buildings.

  • Punch list discipline

    Final walkthrough with a documented punch list—so turnover is clean and predictable.
