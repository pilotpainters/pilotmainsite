The homeowners on this Elmhurst colonial wanted one coordinated finish plan across the kitchen and master bath — without replacing cabinet boxes that were still in great shape. Greige in the kitchen and crisp white on the vanity gave each room its own feel while staying visually connected.

We phased the work kitchen-first, then moved to the vanity with the same degrease-and-sand protocol. Agreeable Gray on the kitchen reads warm and modern against quartz counters; Chantilly Lace on the vanity brightens the bath without fighting the existing tile.
