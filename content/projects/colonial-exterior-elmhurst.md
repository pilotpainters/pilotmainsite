The homeowners on this tree-lined Elmhurst block wanted their 1940s colonial to feel brighter without fighting the neighborhood's traditional character. Faded siding, cracked caulk at window heads, and a sun-bleached garage door were the main targets.

We power-washed the full elevation, replaced a short section of rotted porch rail cap, and re-caulked every trim joint before priming bare wood. Accessible Beige on the lap siding reads warm in afternoon light, while Chantilly Lace trim keeps the roofline and porch details sharp from the street.
