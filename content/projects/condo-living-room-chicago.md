This Lincoln Park condo needed a full interior refresh before the owners moved back in after a kitchen remodel. With building elevator reservations and hallway protection requirements, we phased the work room by room and kept odors low with zero-VOC coatings throughout.

We repaired nail pops, skim-coated two accent-wall areas, and sanded all trim before priming. The living and dining spaces received two coats of Classic Gray — a versatile neutral that reads warm in afternoon light — while ceilings and woodwork were finished in Extra White for a clean, gallery-like contrast.
