When this Schaumburg dental practice decided to renovate their 3,100-square-foot clinic, the top priority was zero disruption to their Monday-through-Friday patient schedule. We mobilized a four-person crew over the Thanksgiving holiday weekend — starting Wednesday evening after the last patient left and finishing by Sunday night.

The waiting room received a Brittany Blue accent wall designed to lower patient anxiety the moment they walk in. Treatment rooms and hallways were coated in Passive, a light warm gray that keeps the spaces bright under the clinical LED lighting without the starkness of pure white walls. All paints were zero-VOC to meet the practice's indoor air quality standards.

We masked around every piece of fixed dental equipment, protected cabinetry with plastic sheeting, and cleaned every surface before final walkthrough on Sunday. The practice opened Monday morning to a clinic that looked — and smelled — brand new, with not a single appointment rescheduled.
