The practice administrator needed every exam room and the waiting area updated before a credentialing visit — with no weekday downtime. We mobilized Friday evening and finished Sunday night, using zero-VOC systems throughout.

Passive gray keeps clinical spaces bright without harsh contrast; a Palladian Blue accent in the waiting room softens the first impression for patients. We masked around fixed equipment, protected flooring, and opened Monday with air quality that met the clinic's standards.
