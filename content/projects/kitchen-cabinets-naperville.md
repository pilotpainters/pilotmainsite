This Naperville homeowner wanted to modernize their 1990s oak kitchen without the cost and disruption of a full remodel. The existing cabinets were structurally solid but the honey-oak finish felt dated against the new quartz countertops they had just installed.

We started with thorough degreasing and scuff-sanding of every surface, followed by a high-adhesion bonding primer formulated specifically for factory-finished cabinetry. Two coats of cabinet-grade enamel in Benjamin Moore Simply White delivered the bright, contemporary look the client was after, while a contrasting Extra White on the island added subtle depth.

The result is a kitchen that feels brand new — clean lines, smooth finish, and hardware that pops against the warm white. The homeowners told us it was the single best upgrade they have made to the house.
