The homeowners in this 1940s Evanston colonial wanted their primary bedroom to feel like a spa retreat — calm, clean, and far removed from the loud teal that had been on the walls since the previous owners. They chose Benjamin Moore Sage Wisdom, a muted green with gray undertones that reads tranquil in both morning and evening light.

We patched a handful of nail pops in the ceiling, skim-coated two areas of damaged plaster near the window headers, and sanded every trim surface before priming. The ceiling received two coats of dead-flat white to keep the eye on the walls, and all woodwork was finished in a semi-gloss Extra White for easy wipe-down and a slight reflective contrast.

The walk-in closet interior was painted in Chantilly Lace — one of the brightest whites in the Benjamin Moore lineup — so the space feels twice its actual size under the recessed lighting the homeowners had added.
