The homeowners wanted their 1920s Elmhurst colonial to feel brighter without losing the warmth of the original wood trim. We started with a full walkthrough to align on sheen levels — flat on ceilings, eggshell on walls, and semi-gloss on all woodwork.

After patching plaster cracks in the foyer and dining room, we primed stained areas and applied Agreeable Gray on the main walls. The trim and crown molding were finished in Chantilly Lace for a sharp, classic contrast that makes the architectural details stand out.
