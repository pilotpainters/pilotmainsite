This Lincoln Park homeowner wanted to keep their solid oak layout but lose the 1990s honey finish. With new countertops already in place, cabinet refinishing was the fastest path to a brighter, more modern kitchen without tearing out boxes.

We labeled every door and drawer, degreased high-touch zones, and scuff-sanded for mechanical adhesion before applying a cabinet bonding primer. Two sprayed coats of White Dove on the perimeter and Pure White on the island delivered a smooth, low-texture finish that reads custom — not brushed-on.
