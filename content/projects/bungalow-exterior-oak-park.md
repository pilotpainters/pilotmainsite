This 1920s craftsman bungalow in Oak Park had peeling paint, chalky siding, and trim that was starting to show bare wood in spots. The homeowners wanted a historically sympathetic palette that would stand out on the tree-lined block without feeling trendy.

We power-washed every surface, scraped and sanded loose paint back to a sound edge, primed bare wood with an alkyd primer for maximum adhesion, and caulked all window and door casings. Siding received two coats of Repose Gray — warm enough to complement the brick foundation, cool enough to read as modern. Trim in White Dove keeps the look crisp, and a Naval-blue front door provides a confident focal point from the street.

The owner said the house looks better now than in the listing photos from when they bought it. That is exactly the kind of result we aim for on every exterior project.
