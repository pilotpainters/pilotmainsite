This two-story colonial in Wheaton had not been painted in over a decade. The existing pale yellow siding was faded and chalking, several fascia sections had moisture damage, and the cedar shutters were starting to cup. The homeowners wanted a dramatic change — something bold and modern that would still feel appropriate on their traditional street.

After power-washing and allowing a full dry day, we scraped and sanded every surface. A six-foot section of rotted fascia on the front gable was replaced with primed PVC trim board before we started coating. We applied a full coat of exterior bonding primer followed by two coats of Iron Ore, a rich charcoal that gives the house serious curb appeal. Trim throughout went in Decorator's White for maximum contrast.

The porch ceiling in Watery — a soft blue-green — was the homeowner's special request, inspired by the Southern haint-blue tradition. It catches afternoon light beautifully and gives the front entry a welcoming warmth you would not expect from the darker palette.
