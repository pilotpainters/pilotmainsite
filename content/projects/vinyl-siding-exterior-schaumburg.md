This Schaumburg split-level had factory-faded vinyl and aluminum trim that no longer matched the owner's updated landscaping plan. Painting vinyl requires the right products and surface prep — we walked the elevation together and locked in a charcoal body with clean white trim before the first ladder went up.

We washed chalk and mildew from every surface, scuff-sanded glossy trim, and used a vinyl-bonding primer on previously coated siding. Iron Ore on the body gives the home a modern edge, while Extra White on fascias, garage faces, and porch posts keeps lines crisp through harsh winter cycles.
