This Logan Square two-flat needed more than a quick coat — years of sun and lake-effect moisture had left trim chalking and porch paint peeling back to bare wood. We coordinated with the owner's tuckpointing contractor so masonry repairs were cured before we masked and prepped the woodwork.

After a full soft-wash, we scraped failing paint to a sound edge, sanded glossy trim, and spot-primed bare areas with a bonding primer. Two coats of White Dove on all casings and porch details give the brick a clean, gallery-like frame, while a Naval-blue door and shutters add curb appeal from the sidewalk.
