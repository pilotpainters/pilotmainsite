A property management company needed their 4,200-square-foot tenant suite refreshed before a new lease signing — and they needed it done without disrupting the outgoing tenant's final week of operations. We scheduled three consecutive overnight shifts starting at 6 PM and wrapped by 5 AM each morning.

Walls throughout received Agreeable Gray for a modern, neutral backdrop that photographs well for listing materials. The main conference room got a Hale Navy accent wall to anchor the space, and all trim and ceiling surfaces were touched up in Pure White. We masked IT cable runs, protected flooring with ram-board, and left the space walk-ready each morning.

The suite was lease-ready two days ahead of the property manager's deadline — fully painted, clean, and smelling fresh thanks to the zero-VOC coatings we specified for occupied commercial spaces.
