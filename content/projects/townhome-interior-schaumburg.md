This Schaumburg townhome needed a coordinated repaint across the open main level, upstairs bedrooms, and stair hall — all while the family stayed in the home. We sequenced the work so the kitchen and living areas were usable each evening.

We deglossed and sanded all trim, caulked gaps along the stair stringers, and applied White Dove on the main walls for a soft, modern neutral. Bedrooms received the same palette for consistency, with Pure White on doors and casing for a clean, durable finish in high-touch areas.
