    - id: what-are-cookies
      heading: What Are Cookies
      body: |
        Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, to provide a better browsing experience, and to supply information to the site operators.

        Cookies may be set by the website you are visiting ("first-party cookies") or by third parties whose content or services are embedded on the site ("third-party cookies"). Cookies can remain on your device for varying lengths of time — "session cookies" are deleted when you close your browser, while "persistent cookies" remain until they expire or are manually deleted.

    - id: managing-cookies
      heading: Managing Cookies
      body: |
        You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

        - View what cookies are stored on your device and delete them individually or in bulk.
        - Block cookies from specific or all websites.
        - Set preferences for first-party and third-party cookies separately.
        - Enable notifications when a cookie is being set.

        Please note that disabling or blocking certain cookies may affect the functionality of the Site and your ability to use some features.

        For instructions on managing cookies in common browsers, visit:

        - [Google Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
        - [Mozilla Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enhanced-tracking-protection-firefox-desktop)
        - [Apple Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-sfri11471)
        - [Microsoft Edge](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/manage-cookies-in-microsoft-edge-168dab11-0753-043d-7c16-ede5947fc64d)
