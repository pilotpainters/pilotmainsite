Project Details

This Wicker Park retailer needed a full interior refresh before a seasonal relaunch — but could not close during business hours. We sequenced the job in five overnight shifts, starting after close and leaving the space walk-ready before staff arrived each morning.

Walls in Agreeable Gray give product displays a neutral backdrop; Chantilly Lace on trim and ceilings keeps the space bright under track lighting. We protected fixtures, coordinated with the landlord on access, and handed off a punch-list-free turnover ahead of the opening event.
