Cabinet Refinishing

Pantry & Kitchen Cabinets in Schaumburg

Schaumburg, IL · Completed April 2026

Start Your Project

Project Gallery

Project Details

This Schaumburg townhome kitchen had builder-grade stained cabinets that felt heavy in an otherwise bright open plan. The owners wanted a bold two-tone scheme — navy bases, white uppers — without the cost of custom replacement doors.

We removed doors and drawer fronts for off-site spray work in a controlled setup, then refinished face frames and pantry shelving in place. Naval on the lowers anchors the room; Chantilly Lace uppers keep the space airy. New bar pulls completed the update before a final walkthrough in natural light.
